Connecting You to the Region’s Top Economies

For more than four decades, Transmar has established itself as the leading regional shipping partner.

We understand the fundamental impact shipping has on global trade and recognize the crucial role our operations play in connecting markets, industries, and consumers well beyond the limits of national borders. By facilitating the movement of cargo, we actively participate in the growth of economies, contribute to the development of global supply chains, and help foster global economic integration.

Our expansive network of partnerships, affiliations, and our direct presence in the MENA region’s most prominent ports in Egypt, UAE, KSA, India, Pakistan, Sudan, Jordan, Yemen, Kuwait, Iraq, Qatar, and Djibouti provide our customers with the global know-how and the local on-ground expertise they need to ensure their cargo arrives to its destination safely and efficiently.
